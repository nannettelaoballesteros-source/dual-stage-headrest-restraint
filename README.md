## 1. System Overview
The Dual-Stage Active Airbag Headrest (6PJ-ORE First-Purpose™) is a life-safety occupant kinetic stabilization and rapid depressurization egress assembly engineered for automotive seating structures. Originating from Delta Nannette Omega - Yeoman LLC (DNO-Y) and open-sourced via the BNG Global Humanitarian Foundation (BNG-GHF), the assembly balances cervical spine deceleration stabilization with automated post-collision cabin depressurization and fail-safe zero-power manual egress clearance. Commercial and industrial mechanics remain under separate license with Ballesteros Ballast Infrastructure LLC (BBI).

---

## 2. Core Functional Features
* **Dynamic Deceleration Stabilization (Stage 1):** Automatically translates and tilts forward along a defined kinematic arc upon primary deceleration, cupping the occipital-cervical junction (C1–C7) to arrest whiplash and rebound shear forces.
* **Rearward Kinetic Barrier Lobe:** Deploys an expansive rearward barrier envelope down the seatback plane to intercept aft-seated passengers and prevent footwell ejection during secondary collisions.
* **Secondary Rapid Dump Evacuation (Stage 2):** Automated active bypass port reduces internal gauge pressure to zero within 500 to 1,500 milliseconds post-impact, collapsing the composite textile flush against the seat frame to clear an unobstructed extraction corridor.
* **Zero-Power Tri-Point Egress Override:** Incorporates triple independent manual mechanical release interfaces (port flank, starboard flank, and apex crown) with dual-stage sequential pull-tabs for non-electric rescue extraction.
* **Independent Anterior Cervical Airway Release:** A dedicated single-stage manual pull actuator along the forward cheek margin immediately dumps cervical pressure, freeing the occupant's neck for rapid endotracheal intubation and rigid cervical collar application.
